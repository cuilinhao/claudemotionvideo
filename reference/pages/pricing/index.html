Pricing questions

01

Can I buy one video without a subscription?

Registration includes 0 free videos, and no card binding is required to create an account. Pay $5 USD once for one new 10, 15 or 30-second video generation, including up to 4 reference images, 1080p MP4 export, built-in music and audio uploads. No subscription or automatic renewal. Extra edits and AI voiceover use subscription credits. Monthly and yearly subscriptions remain available; prices are in US dollars.

02

How many Opus 5.5 videos do I get?

With subscription credits, a 10-second video costs 80 credits, 15 seconds costs 100 and 30 seconds costs 160. Each chat edit costs 20. A video made from your own images costs 60 more for the first image and 20 for each more, up to four (and 40 per edit, plus 20 for each image an edit adds), because your image inputs are sent to the model for each generation or edit. So Starter's 1,500 credits make about 15 15-second videos a month.

03

How do yearly plans work?

You pay once a year and save up to 50% against monthly billing (30% on Starter, 40% on Pro, 50% on Max). Starter is $166.8 a year, and your credits still arrive every month.

04

What happens if a generation fails?

If it fails on our side, or Opus tells you it can't make what you asked, the credits or purchased generation go back to your balance automatically. Closing or refreshing the page doesn't stop a generation; it finishes on our servers and is saved to My videos.

05

Do unused credits expire?

Credits refresh every month, including on yearly plans. Unused ones roll over to the next month, up to 2× your plan's monthly amount.

06

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Switch plans right here on the pricing page, or cancel from your account page. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

07

Is there a refund?

If you haven't used any credits from a charge, contact us within 7 days to request a refund review. Requests are reviewed manually and approval is not automatic. See the refund policy for details.

08

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes, on every plan.
