What is an Opus 5.5 video?

An Opus 5.5 video is a short animation written by Claude Opus 5.5, an AI model from Anthropic. Opus 5.5 isn't a video model: it takes text and images in and writes text out. So instead of generating pixels like a diffusion model, it writes the video as code (canvas drawing, typography, 3D scenes and precise timing), and that code is played back and recorded frame by frame into an MP4.

That's why an Opus 5.5 video is so good for motion graphics, product launches, app walkthroughs, explainers and data stories. Typography, colours and image placement are controlled by generated code. Uploads remain available as visual references, and each edit creates a new version; results can vary, so review the output.

Making one used to mean setting up Claude, a render script and ffmpeg yourself. Claude Motion Video does it all in your browser: describe the video or add a screenshot, watch the storyboard appear, refine it in chat, and download the MP4.

Opus 5.5 video vs AI video models

Opus 5.5 is great at

Kinetic type, UI and app demos, charts and data stories, logo reveals, product ads from your own images, 3D motion graphics. Anything where text and layout must be exact.

Use a video model for

Live-action scenes, realistic people and faces, camera moves through a photoreal world. Sora, Veo and Seedance paint pixels; Opus 5.5 writes code.

Only Opus can do this

Change one thing and keep the rest. Because the video is code, "make the title bigger" changes the title, not a new random take.

Opus 5.5 video FAQ

01

What is the Opus 5.5 video generator?

Claude Motion Video is an Opus 5.5 video generator and editor. You describe the video you want or add a screenshot, Claude Opus 5.5 plans a storyboard and writes every frame as code, and you refine it in chat and export an MP4, all in your browser.

02

Can Claude Opus 5.5 really make videos?

Yes, but not by painting pixels. Opus 5.5 takes text and images in and writes text out, so it writes the animation as code, and that code is played back and recorded frame by frame into an MP4. That's why text stays sharp, brand colours stay exact, and you can change one thing without regenerating the whole video.

03

Do I need a Claude subscription or API key?

No. We run Claude Opus 5.5 for you. Pick a plan, describe the video, and download the MP4.

04

What kinds of Opus 5.5 videos can I make?

Product launch videos, app walkthroughs, mobile game ads, motion design showreels, logo reveals, explainers, data stories, 9:16 social ads and kinetic typography. You can also use your own screenshot or product image as a visual reference for code-based motion; objects are not automatically separated into layers.

05

What can't an Opus 5.5 video do?

Live-action footage, realistic people, faces and bodies moving, or anything photoreal. For those, native video models like Sora, Veo or Seedance are the right tool. Opus 5.5 is a motion designer that writes code: type, UI, charts, shapes, 3D scenes and your own images.

06

How long does an Opus 5.5 video take to make?

Usually a minute or two. The storyboard appears within seconds, then Opus 5.5 writes the code for each scene. Changes by chat usually take under half a minute.

07

How long can an Opus 5.5 video be?

Up to 30 seconds per video, in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, exported as a 1080p MP4.

08

Is Claude Motion Video made by Anthropic?

No. Claude Motion Video is an independent product built on Claude Opus 5.5. When newer Claude models ship, we'll add them.
