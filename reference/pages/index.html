06 · Under the hood

Why your text and UI stay sharp

AI video models paint every frame as pixels, so they redraw your interface, blur small text and give you a new random take each time you ask for a change. That's fine for live-action scenes, and wrong for a product video.

AI motion graphics work the other way. In Claude Motion Video, Claude Opus 5.5 writes code that animates your uploaded image and renders typography and shapes frame by frame. Your image remains available as a visual reference, and chat edits create a new version that you can review.

08 · FAQ

AI motion graphics FAQ

01

What are AI motion graphics?

Animated graphics (moving type, UI, charts, logos, shapes and product shots) made by AI from a description or an image. Claude Motion Video makes AI motion graphics with Claude Opus 5.5, which writes every frame as code, so text stays sharp and colours stay exact.

02

What is Claude Motion Video?

An AI motion graphics generator you can edit precisely. Turn a screenshot, a product photo or one sentence into motion graphics (an app promo, a game ad, a product launch, a data story), then change any part by telling Claude Motion Video what you want. Export a 1080p MP4.

03

Can I turn my own screenshot into motion graphics?

Yes. Add a screenshot, game screen, product photo or poster and say what should move: spin the wheel, pop the reward, draw the chart, sweep a shine across the bottle. Claude uses the uploaded image as visual input when writing animation code. The product does not automatically isolate people or objects into separate layers. If a generation is marked failed before a video is saved, its credits are returned automatically. You can use up to four images in one video; the first adds 60 credits and each more 20.

04

How do I change just one part?

Type the change, like "make the headline bigger" or "slow the chart down". Edits create a new version. To focus a change, pick a shot on the timeline and describe the requested edit in chat. Every version is kept, so you can compare or go back.

05

How are AI motion graphics different from AI video generators like Sora or Veo?

Video models generate pixels, while Claude Motion Video uses code for typography, shapes and timing and can use your image as a visual reference. The result may vary with your prompt, so review each saved version. For live-action or realistic people, a video model is the better tool.

06

What kinds of motion graphics can I make?

App launch videos, product walkthroughs, mobile game ads, flash-sale ads, logo reveals, kinetic typography, explainers, data stories and 9:16 social posts, in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, up to 30 seconds.

07

Which AI makes the motion graphics?

Claude Opus 5.5 plans and writes every video. See how it works on our Opus 5.5 video generator page.

08

Can I use the motion graphics commercially?

Yes. Everything you make on Claude Motion Video is yours to use, including in ads and client work.
